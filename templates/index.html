Delivery Time Engine

Machine Learning Model utilizing 14 real-time data parameters to calculate exact ETA.

  • Age & Ratings Delivery personnel biometrics and historical performance data.
  • Geospatial Data Exact GPS Lat/Long mapping for Restaurant and Destination.
  • Time Deltas Algorithmic calculation based on Order Time vs. Pickup Time.
  • Weather Logic Adjusts for Sunny, Stormy, Windy, Cloudy, Fog, or Sandstorms.
  • Traffic Density Real-time routing adjustment (Low, Medium, High, Jam).
  • Vehicle Telemetry Vehicle type (Scooter/Bike/EV) and physical condition rating (0-5).
  • Order Metrics Analyzes load constraints (Snack, Meal, Drinks, Buffet).
  • Logistics Accounts for multiple grouped deliveries (0, 1, 2+) and Festival surges.
  • City Zoning Calibrated for Metropolitan, Urban, and Semi-Urban infrastructures.
  • Distance Total route distance calculated in Kilometers.
